# Content guardrails

## Safe language

Prefer direct, defensible wording such as:

- Quality-led systems selected for compatibility, suitability, and support.
- A solution designed around your operating requirements.
- Clear scope, assumptions, responsibilities, and payment stages.
- Warranty support depends on the approved product and manufacturer terms.
- We can assess your site and recommend the appropriate next step.
- Commercial and selected residential solutions.
- Monitoring and maintenance options are available.

## Never invent

Do not publish unsupported:

- project or customer counts;
- savings percentages;
- response-time promises;
- years of experience;
- certifications or licences;
- official partnership or manufacturer-authorisation status;
- warranty durations;
- system capacities or technical specifications;
- addresses, telephone numbers, WhatsApp numbers, or email addresses;
- testimonials;
- named projects, locations, clients, or results.

## Missing information

When a business-specific value is missing:

1. Search repository documentation, environment examples, CMS/data sources, and supplied assets.
2. If still unavailable, do not fabricate it.
3. Hide the optional public element or use a centralized internal configuration value that is not rendered until verified.
4. Add the item to `CLIENT_APPROVAL_CHECKLIST.md`.
5. Explain the gap in `HANDOVER.md`.

Do not display `lorem ipsum`, bracketed placeholders, fake testimonials, sample contact details, or fabricated metrics on public pages.

## Products and projects

- Use real supplied product images and information.
- Do not display public prices unless explicitly approved.
- Do not invent specifications.
- When specifications are incomplete, present category-level benefits and request a technical consultation.
- Use genuine project imagery where available.
- Without verified case-study facts, present capabilities, process, or installation environments instead of fictional projects.
