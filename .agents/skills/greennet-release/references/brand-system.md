# GreenNet brand system

## Identity

- Legal/long form: GreenNet Energy Ltd
- Everyday brand: GreenNet
- Primary tagline: Powering Smarter Futures.

## Approved positioning

GreenNet Energy provides quality solar systems, installation, monitoring, and support for dependable power infrastructure.

GreenNet helps commercial, institutional, industrial, and selected large residential clients invest in dependable renewable-energy systems through quality product selection, professional installation, monitoring, and after-sales support.

## Primary audiences

- Commercial and industrial organisations
- Factories and offices
- Hotels and institutions
- Property developers
- Selected premium or large residential customers

## Visual direction

Colours:

- Midnight Navy: `#0D1B24`
- Deep Petrol Teal: `#0C5A56`
- Solar Amber: `#F3B23F`
- Titanium Grey: `#69757A`
- Warm White: `#F5F2EA`

Suggested balance:

- 55% Warm White
- 25% Midnight Navy
- 12% Deep Petrol Teal
- 6% Titanium Grey
- 2% Solar Amber

Typography, when already available or safe to add through the project’s existing font strategy:

- Space Grotesk for display headings
- Inter for body copy, labels, navigation, forms, and technical information

Use controlled geometry, technical grids, energy-flow paths, monitoring signals, and restrained amber highlights. Avoid generic eco-template styling, excessive green effects, decorative leaves, uncontrolled gradients, and animation that reduces clarity.

## Logo policy

Search the repository before changing logo usage. Prefer:

1. approved final SVG assets;
2. approved final high-resolution PNG assets;
3. the latest consistent logo implementation already used in the production code;
4. a clean text wordmark only when approved artwork is absent.

Do not invent, redraw, or reinterpret the logo. Use consistent light, dark/reversed, monochrome, favicon, and social variants only when those assets exist or can be safely derived without changing the mark.
