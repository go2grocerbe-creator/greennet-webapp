How It Works

From Enquiry to Illumination

A clear, guided journey from your first call to a fully commissioned solar system.

  1. 01

    Free Site Assessment

    Our engineers visit your property to evaluate your energy needs, roof or ground conditions, and available solar resource.

  2. 02

    Custom System Design

    We design a solar system precisely tailored to your energy consumption profile and budget, specifying the right products for your application.

  3. 03

    Professional Installation

    Our trained technicians carry out the full installation — panels, inverters, batteries and wiring — safely and efficiently, with minimal disruption.

  4. 04

    Commissioning & Ongoing Support

    We commission and test the system, walk you through its operation, and remain available for continued maintenance and after-sales support.
